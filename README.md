## Book

I'm currently working on a book, more properly a general guide at present, on creating and maintaining technical documentation. I plan to cover content strategy, content structure and governance, publishing, task analysis, and analytics (and probably other topics too). I work on adding content as time and family life allow. It's very much work in progress; see my progress at [Technical Documentation: From Basics to Complex Systems](https://johnkinsky.github.io/book/).

Once finished, the guide is intended to provide useful practices, methodologies, and tools to anyone who wants to create content or documentation, reduce publishing and review friction, understand effective content metrics, and track content effectiveness in real time.
